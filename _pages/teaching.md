  <section class="teaching-philosophy" aria-labelledby="teaching-philosophy-title">
    <div>
      <p class="teaching-eyebrow">TEACHING APPROACH</p>
      <h2 id="teaching-philosophy-title">A connected engineering curriculum</h2>
    </div>
    <p>I value clear physical intuition, reproducible computation, and purposeful experimentation. Across introductory and advanced courses, the goal is the same: make complex ideas approachable without losing their rigor.</p>
  </section>
